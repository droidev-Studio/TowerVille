## Game Overview

**TowerVille** combines three ideas:

- **Magic Tower strategy** — move tile by tile, manage HP, attack, defense, coins, and keys.
- **Roguelite runs** — climb through a 10-floor tower with blessings, events, shops, and changing rewards.
- **Cozy animal style** — pastel colors, cute creatures, toy-like tiles, gentle feedback, and a friendly storybook world.

The result is a simple but strategic browser game:

> A non-scary Magic Tower roguelite for cozy game lovers.

## How to Play

Start from the town, enter the storybook tower, and climb floor by floor.

On each floor, choose your route carefully:

- Fight tower critters for rewards.
- Pick up hearts, attack flowers, shield leaves, and bell pouches.
- Spend keys to open doors.
- Visit events and shops.
- Reach the stairs to climb higher.
- Choose blessings every few floors.
- Defeat the boss on the final floor.

The core question is always:

> Should I spend HP, keys, or coins now — or save them for later?

## Game Feel

TowerVille is designed to feel:

- cute
- warm
- strategic
- easy to understand
- non-violent
- cozy but still challenging

Instead of scary monsters, the tower is filled with playful creatures and storybook obstacles. Instead of fast action combat, the fun comes from planning the best path through each floor.
